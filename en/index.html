How it works

STEP 1

Pick a sample or a blank page

Choose a design on the left. Five designs are free; the others come with Pro.

STEP 2

Type over the page

Click any text on the page and type. Hover an item or category for add, reorder and delete buttons. Put the Japanese in the main line and the English in the subtitle — or the other way round. Pick the Japanese and Latin typefaces separately under Fonts, and the currency under Layout ($, £, € and ¥).

STEP 3

Save as PDF

Press PDF / Print, choose Save as PDF and tick Background graphics. The page comes out exactly A4. On US Letter paper, choose Fit to page.

Check the Japanese before you print. Machine translation often mangles dish names. A quick look from a Japanese-speaking colleague or supplier saves a reprint.

Questions

Is MenuFits free?

Yes. Making a menu and saving it as an A4 PDF is free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on exports. MenuFits Pro is an optional one-time upgrade for more designs, covers and booklets.

Do I need to read Japanese?

No. The editor is in English. You type or paste the Japanese names yourself and choose the Japanese and Latin typefaces separately. Have a Japanese speaker check dish names before printing.

Can I print on US Letter paper?

Pages are A4. On US Letter paper, choose Fit to page in the print dialog (about 94%).

Where is my menu stored?

Only in your browser. Nothing you type or upload is sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data clears the menu too, so save a .json file as a backup.

Can I use the menus commercially?

Yes. Print and use them in your restaurant freely. Please do not resell or redistribute the tool itself.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Pro is not right for you, ask within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. A refunded key can no longer be activated on new devices. Details are in our terms.

Can I use Pro in both Chrome and Edge?

Yes. Enter the same MenuFits license key in each browser. Chrome and Edge on the same computer use two of your five activation slots. Menus do not sync automatically between browsers: save a JSON file and open it in the other browser. MenuFits and MiseFits have separate license keys.

What happens if I clear browser data or lose my key?

Clearing site data removes locally saved menus and Pro activation. Keep a JSON backup and your license email. Entering your key again uses a new activation slot; if you reach the five-slot limit, contact our support form with the email address used for purchase. Private browsing may not retain your work after the session ends. Do not publish your license key or payment confirmation URL.

Which browser should I use?

We recommend the latest Chrome or Edge on a computer. Typefaces load from the internet, so use MenuFits online. If saving or printing does not work inside a social or other app, open this site directly in Chrome or Edge.

For PDF export, enable Background graphics and select A4, no margins and 100% scale. On US Letter, select Fit to page instead. Auto-saved work is separate on each device and browser; save a JSON file as a backup.
